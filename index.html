⚠️ Storm & Fall Hazard Safety Notice

Do NOT climb onto a wet, damaged, or steep-pitch roof. Falls cause severe injuries. If you see sagging ceilings or water pooling near electrical fixtures, turn off power to that circuit at the breaker panel and place buckets below the drip until a professional arrives.

Common Tampa Bay Roof Issues We Connect

Emergency Leak & Tarping

Rapid tarping stops water infiltration into attics and drywall during active storm cycles, preventing costly mold and structural damage.

Wind & Shingle Blow-Off

High coastal wind gusts lift asphalt tabs, breaking the sealant bond and exposing underlayment to driving rain.

Flashing & Valley Leaks

Chimneys, skylights, plumbing vents, and roof intersections are frequent leak points when sealants crack under intense Florida sunshine.

Tile & Flat Roof Repairs

Cracked concrete or clay barrel tiles and ponding water on modified bitumen or TPO flat sections require specialized commercial repair expertise.

Repair vs. Replacement: What to Consider

  • Age of Current Roof: Architectural shingles in Florida typically last 15–20 years. If your roof is under 12 years old and damage is localized, a targeted repair is often the most economical choice.
  • Extent of Storm Damage: Florida building codes (the 25% rule) may require full roof replacement if over 25% of the roof section is compromised within a 12-month period. An experienced roofer will inspect the total area.
  • Insurance Documentation: If damage is storm-related, photograph all interior water marks and exterior shingle damage immediately before emergency repairs are begun.
